### Why my stack looks like this

Most Laravel developers can wire up a CRUD app. Fewer have sat with a webhook that fires twice, or a gateway sandbox that lies about what production will do. My work has been shaped by payments — where "it works on my machine" isn't good enough, because a bug means someone's donation or cashback silently disappears. That's the standard I hold everything else to, backend or front.
